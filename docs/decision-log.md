| 71 | 2026-11-22 | Bracket and quote matching rules | **Autonomous default** (#35). With the caret inside a string, its two quotes are boxed; otherwise a bracket right before the caret (or, failing that, right after it) is boxed with its partner. Brackets inside strings are skipped. An unclosed string or an unbalanced or mismatched bracket (`[` closed by `}`) shows nothing. The boxes are painted over the text in the theme's tertiary colour, because re_editor has no way to style single characters by caret position. To change: rules in `lib/document/bracket_match.dart`; look in `_PairPainter` (`lib/ui/text_pane.dart`). |
| 72 | 2026-11-22 | Folding shows one line | **Autonomous default** (#36). | 75 | 2026-11-22 | Key autocomplete details | **Autonomous default** (#40). Suggestions appear when a quote is typed where an object key goes (after `{`, `,` or at the start of a line, inside an object, not an array). | 76 | 2026-11-22 | Text-mode search rules | **Autonomous default** (#41).
